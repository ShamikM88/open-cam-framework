# Shared glossary

Short definitions for terms used in OpenCAM's UK credit workflows. Where meaning depends on an agreement, the agreement and source documents take precedence.

- **CAM (Credit Assessment Memorandum):** The written assessment of a proposed lending decision, covering the borrower, key figures, risks and recommendation.
- **Charge:** Legal security over an asset or other property, giving a lender the rights set out in the security document if obligations are not met.
- **Covenant:** A promise or financial test in a lending agreement that the parties are expected to meet; the agreement sets out the consequences of breach.
- **DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio):** EBITDA divided by interest paid plus scheduled principal repayments over the same period; it indicates how earnings cover scheduled debt service.
- **EBITDA:** Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation; a common measure of operating earnings, not the same as cash available for debt service.
- **Finance lease:** An asset-finance arrangement in which a customer pays to use an asset over an agreed term; ownership and end-of-term responsibilities depend on the contract.
- **GBB (guaranteed buy back):** A vendor's promise to repurchase specified unsold or repossessed stock; unlike an RV guarantee, it is a promise to buy back the stock rather than simply support its end-of-term value.
- **Guarantee:** A commitment to meet specified obligations if the party primarily responsible does not; the extent of the commitment depends on the wording.
- **KYC-AML (Know Your Customer / anti-money laundering):** Identity and risk checks intended to establish who a customer is and help detect or prevent financial crime.
- **LGD (Loss Given Default):** The estimated proportion of an exposure that will not be recovered after default, usually expressed as a percentage.
- **PD (Probability of Default):** The estimated likelihood that a borrower will default over a stated period.
- **PSC (Person with Significant Control):** A UK company-law term for a person who meets the criteria for significant ownership or control of a company.
- **Residual value (RV):** The estimated value of a financed asset at the end of the finance term; an RV guarantee backs that value, whereas a GBB requires the vendor to repurchase specified stock under the agreed terms.
- **TNW (Tangible Net Worth):** Total equity minus intangible assets; it measures net worth without relying on items such as goodwill.
- **UBO (Ultimate Beneficial Owner):** The person who ultimately owns or controls a company, directly or through an ownership chain.
